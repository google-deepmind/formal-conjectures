## Analytic countermodel with the same moment constraints

The following is an analytic argument, not a Lean theorem about the
repository's Weil form. It shows that the proposed abstract ingredients
do not imply the missing compatibility of positive approximations.
It neither disproves RH nor disproves the proposed AEGIS arbitrary
finite-shift theorem.

For complex-valued smooth compactly supported `f`, define

\[
 E(f)=\int_{\mathbb R}|f(x)|^2\,dx,\qquad
 Q_*(f)=\int_{\mathbb R}|f'(x)|^2\,dx
             -\left|\int_{\mathbb R}f(x)\,dx\right|^2.
\]

This is a translation-invariant quadratic form. It is continuous in
`H^1 ∩ L^1` with norm `||f||_2 + ||f'||_2 + ||f||_1`, and consequently
under smooth convergence on a common compact support. Translation is
continuous in this topology. The subgroup generated by `log 2` and
`log 3` is dense, independently of this form.

### Every fixed finite shift family is positive at a narrow enough width

Fix distinct shifts `d_1,...,d_N`, with `N ≥ 1`. Let `g` be supported
in an interval of half-width `r > 0`, and choose `2r` smaller than every
nonzero shift gap. The translated supports are pairwise disjoint.
For coefficients `z_i` and `F = sum z_i g(.-d_i)`, disjointness gives

\[
 \|F'\|_2^2=\|g'\|_2^2\sum_i|z_i|^2,
 \qquad \int F=(\int g)\sum_i z_i.
\]

Cauchy--Schwarz gives `|integral g|^2 ≤ 2r E(g)` and
`|sum z_i|^2 ≤ N sum |z_i|^2`. The fundamental theorem of calculus
from the left endpoint, followed by Cauchy--Schwarz, gives
`E(g) ≤ 4r^2 ||g'||_2^2`. Therefore

\[
 Q_*(F)\ge
 \left(\frac1{4r^2}-2Nr\right)E(g)\sum_i|z_i|^2.
\]

Take `r ≤ 1/(4N)` in addition to the separation bound. The displayed
coefficient is positive. This works for every mother packet at that
width, including every packet with both exponential moments zero.
The zero-dimensional case is immediate.

The countermodel even has a divergent local diagonal floor:
`Q_*(g) ≥ (1/(4r^2)-2r) E(g)`. For separated translates its cross
term has absolute value `|integral g|^2 ≤ 2r E(g)`.
Thus a growing diagonal floor and bounded fixed-gap interactions do
not fix the quantifier problem.

### The full moment-zero space nevertheless has negative directions

Choose any nonnegative, nonzero real `phi ∈ C_c^infinity(R)`. For
`R > 0`, put

\[
 \phi_R(x)=\phi(x/R),\qquad
 h_R=(D^2-1/4)\phi_R.
\]

Two integrations by parts, with zero boundary terms, give

\[
 \int e^{x/2}h_R(x)\,dx=0,
 \qquad \int e^{-x/2}h_R(x)\,dx=0.
\]

Set `A=||phi'''||_2^2`, `B=||phi''||_2^2`, `C=||phi'||_2^2`, and
`M=integral phi > 0`. Change of variables and integration by parts
give the exact identity

\[
 Q_*(h_R)
 =\frac{A}{R^5}+\frac{B}{2R^3}+\frac{C}{16R}
      -\frac{R^2M^2}{16}.
\]

This is strictly negative for sufficiently large `R`: after division
by `R^2`, the first three terms tend to zero and the last term is
`-M^2/16 < 0`. No numerical premise is used.

## Exact implication that fails

Finite-family positivity with a separately selected width, moment-zero
differential generators, dense shift locations, and continuity all hold
in this countermodel. Universal positivity fails. The missing step
must therefore prove a sequence of *certified positive packets* that
approximates each desired packet in a topology where the actual Weil
quadratic converges. Narrowing widths after fixing each finite shift
family does not establish that sequence. Neither does density of
the shift locations.

The source-bound obligation in `RHCertifiedApproximationV1.lean`
states this compatibility in terms of convergence of the actual
arithmetic-form values. Its closure theorem uses the existing dyadic
nonpositivity producers and the closedness of the nonpositive real
half-line, then the verified equivalence with zero-quadratic
nonnegativity. This note supplies no proof of that obligation.
